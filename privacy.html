VisitBrief supportPrivacy

Your notes, under your control.

Effective October 22, 2026. This policy describes VisitBrief for iPhone, provided by Christianbrain.

Information stored on your iPhone

VisitBrief stores symptom entries, symptom-free check-ins, appointment notes, questions, highlights, templates, favorites, and drafts in the app’s local storage. The app has no user account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, health-data server, or HealthKit integration. We do not receive your journal or use it for advertising, profiling, model training, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.

Optional speech and local organization

When you choose Record on this device, VisitBrief requests microphone and Speech permission and requests on-device English speech recognition only. There is no server-based speech fallback. The app does not persist an audio file. The initial transcript, your corrections, additional answers and confirmed excerpts are saved as local journal text only after you confirm and save the entry. Speech can mishear you, so review it before saving.

On supported iPhones running iOS 26 or later, an available Apple on-device language model may organize literal excerpts. Basic text matching and manual questions remain available when the model is unavailable, declines or fails, and you can choose basic matching yourself. VisitBrief has no developer AI server and does not send your story to a cloud AI service. Apple device services and system diagnostics remain subject to Apple policies and your settings. You can deny microphone or Speech permission and type instead. These features do not diagnose illness or estimate disease risk.

Backup and exports

The journal folder is excluded from automatic device backup. You can export an encrypted backup using a passphrase you control. We cannot recover a lost passphrase. PDF and CSV exports are readable files. Files you save to a cloud provider, send through another app, or share with a clinician are handled by the recipients and providers you choose under their policies. Generated exports are removed after 24 hours when another export is created, or when you delete all notes. Copies saved outside VisitBrief remain until you delete them there.

Device features and Apple

Optional device authentication is handled by iOS; VisitBrief does not receive biometric data. Local reminders contain generic text. Apple handles payments and purchase status and may process purchase and diagnostic data under its own policies and your system settings. VisitBrief does not receive card details.

Support correspondence

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you send so we can respond to the request. Do not send health information, backups, passphrases, or payment details. Request deletion of your support correspondence by replying to the support email; legal retention obligations may apply. We do not sell support correspondence.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit and delete journal entries, remove reminders, and delete all local notes in Settings. Deleting local notes does not remove exported copies held elsewhere or refund an Apple purchase. We cannot retrieve journal content from your device.

Adults and medical boundaries

VisitBrief is intended for adults aged 18 or older. It is a personal record and appointment-preparation tool and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency care.

This support website

This site uses no cookies, analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking pixels added by Christianbrain. If published on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes connection information such as IP addresses under its own privacy statement.

Contact and changes

For privacy or app support requests, email christianbrain@gmail.com. Please do not include medical details. Changes to this policy will be reflected here with a revised effective date.
